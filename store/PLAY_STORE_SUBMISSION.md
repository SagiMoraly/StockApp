## Full Description

StockUp helps you manage grocery planning without extra clutter.

Create your own pantry item catalog, build recipes, and add ingredients directly into your grocery list. Everything stays simple, fast, and focused on the essentials.

With StockUp you can:

- Organize staple items by category
- Create and edit your own recipes
- Add recipe ingredients directly to a grocery list
- Track what you still need to buy
- Keep your data stored locally on your device

StockUp does not require an account. Photo access is optional and only used if you choose to attach an image to a recipe.
