# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-27

StockUp stores your grocery lists, pantry items, favorites, and recipes locally on your device.

## Information We Collect

StockUp does not require account registration and does not collect personal information for remote storage.

If you choose to add an image to a recipe, StockUp requests access to your device photo library only for that action.

## How Information Is Used

- Grocery lists, pantry items, recipes, and favorites are stored locally on your device.
- Recipe images selected from your device are used only inside the app.

## Data Sharing

StockUp does not share your data with third parties.

## Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you edit, remove, or uninstall the app.

## Your Choices

- You can use StockUp without creating an account.
- You can deny photo access and continue using the app without recipe images.

## Contact

Support email: support@stockup.app
